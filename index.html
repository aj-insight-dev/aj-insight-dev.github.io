    NO AUDIO · GOOD AI CONCEPT

    GENTLE AUGMENTATION

    AI should support the keeper—not pretend to be the bonsai expert.

    The most interesting AI direction became diagnostic restraint: organize observations, separate signal from noise, keep uncertainty visible and help formulate better questions.

    OBSERVE what the keeper actually seesCOMPARE signals without declaring certaintyGUARDRAIL keep evidence and unknowns visibleHUMAN expert judgment stays authoritative
    PAUSED · NOT ABANDONED

    THE EXPERT-VALIDATION GATE

    The tree is still growing.

    I could keep generating features. I could not responsibly decide by myself which ones would actually help bonsai keepers.
